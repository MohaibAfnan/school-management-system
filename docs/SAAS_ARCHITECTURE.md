# School Management SaaS Architecture

## Product direction

Keep Laravel, Inertia, Vue, and the existing school-scoped data model. Move to a conventional self-service SaaS onboarding model. A public visitor creates an account, verifies email with a short-lived one-time code, receives a school workspace and owner account in one transaction, starts a seven-day trial, and then enters the dashboard. No developer-issued activation key or school code is required for owner sign-in.

## Tenancy and identity

- A school workspace is the tenant and remains the boundary for all academic, finance, and people records.
- Signup creates the workspace and its first owner atomically. Never accept a client-supplied `school_id`, role, subscription state, or trial expiry.
- Authenticate by verified email and password. Resolve the tenant from the authenticated user's server-side membership; never trust a school code supplied in a login form to select another tenant.
- Keep workspace codes for invitations, support, and future multi-workspace selection, not as a required login factor.
- Platform operators remain on the separate, non-publicly linked `/developer` guard and control plane.

## Signup and verification

1. Collect school name, owner name, email, password, and acceptance of terms/privacy policy.
2. Normalize email; enforce uniqueness and strong password rules; throttle registration and code requests.
3. Create a pending owner account and workspace transactionally, with an opaque workspace slug/code generated by the server.
4. Send a cryptographically random six-digit email OTP through the configured transport. Store only a hash, expiry, attempt counter, and resend throttle. Never log or persist the plaintext code.
5. Verify the OTP, mark the email verified, start the seven-day trial, establish the authenticated session, and redirect to the dashboard.
6. Password reset uses the verified email identity and does not ask the user for a school code.

If email delivery is unavailable, show a clear retry state and do not claim verification or grant a verified owner session. Local development may use Laravel's log mailer; hosted SaaS must configure a reliable provider.

## Trial and billing

- Start the seven-day clock after successful email verification, not when an abandoned form is first opened.
- Store `trial_ends_at` server-side and calculate access from subscription state on every protected request.
- During trial, provide the full product experience with clear remaining-time messaging.
- After expiry, retain all school data and allow sign-in, read-only access, export, billing, and support. Block mutations through a central entitlement middleware/policy, not scattered UI checks.
- Paid plans should be enrollment-based tiers with included seats/storage, transparent overage rules, and feature entitlements. Offer annual billing at a discount and monthly billing for flexibility. Keep prices configurable by currency/market rather than hard-coded.
- Premium should emphasize operational value: unlimited core records within the chosen tier, advanced reporting, automated backups/retention, parent/teacher mobile access, LMS, audit history, priority support, and integrations. Avoid charging separately for basic login, OTP, or essential data export.
- Do not collect card data during the trial unless a payment provider and local compliance review are in place. At expiry, prompt for upgrade and preserve data.

## Authorization and data safety

- Keep tenant scoping in server-side query scopes and add tenant ownership checks to every route, job, notification, file path, and export.
- The first workspace user gets the owner/admin role from server code. Additional staff and student accounts are provisioned by school administrators with least-privilege role grants.
- Use policy checks for feature entitlements and role permissions; navigation visibility is not authorization.
- Audit billing, ownership, role, and tenant lifecycle changes. Never record passwords, OTPs, reset tokens, SMTP secrets, or full payment data.
- Plan migration/backfill and tenant-isolation checks before production rollout; preserve existing schools and accounts.

## Rollout sequence

1. Add automated regression coverage for tenant isolation, signup concurrency, OTP expiry/reuse, throttling, and trial boundaries.
2. Build signup and email OTP flow with atomic tenant/owner creation.
3. Replace school-code login with email identity and server-side tenant resolution; support multiple memberships when introduced.
4. Add trial lifecycle, entitlements, upgrade checkout, and post-expiry read-only access.
5. Migrate existing schools to explicit subscription states without changing their accounts or passwords; retain the developer portal as an internal control plane.
6. Remove the old activation-key flow only after hosted and localhost install modes have a documented support path.

## Rollout guardrails

- Keep the current `/login` route compatible during migration and provide redirects for saved links.
- Do not deploy a schema or auth cutover until migration, rollback, tenant-isolation, and mail delivery checks pass.
- Use feature flags for self-service registration and trial enforcement until the new flow passes end-to-end checks in a non-production environment.
